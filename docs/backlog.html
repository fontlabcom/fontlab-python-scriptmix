The other 69 sample originals also retain their historical identities. The sample migration denominator stays 130; released acceptance remains 68/130, with the Italic Angle candidate and these 61 ports still subject to their existing gates. The wider Glyphs source corpus, PythonQt adaptations, package cleanup and native acceptance remain in scope.
